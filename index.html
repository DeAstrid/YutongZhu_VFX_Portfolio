Summary

Recently graduated VFX artist with a strong technical background in Physics and Computer Science. My primary focus is procedural simulation and tool creation in Houdini. I complement this with secondary expertise in Nuke compositing and broad training across the wider VFX pipeline, including creature modeling, texturing and rigging. I aim to bridge the gap between creative vision and complex technical systems and build grounded, tangible visuals and efficient, reliable workflows for production.
